## How to choose a Christmas gift for your boyfriend's dad

Start with one question for your boyfriend: what does his dad do on a Saturday? Grills, fishes, reads, watches the game, hosts the neighbors? Then buy a premium version of the gear he already uses. A gift tied to a hobby shows you listened, and it's far safer than clothing, cologne or anything with a joke on it.

Keep it classy and shareable. Gifts the whole family can enjoy, like a fire pit, a cocktail maker or a speaker, take the pressure off and make a good first impression. If you've only been dating a few months, a mid-range gift like the MEATER Plus is plenty; save the Traeger for a joint gift with your boyfriend.

## Is it okay to give your boyfriend's dad a gift for Christmas?

Yes. If you're spending the holidays with his family, bringing a gift for each parent is a kind gesture, and a handwritten card matters as much as the gift.
