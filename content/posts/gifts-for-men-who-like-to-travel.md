## What do you buy a man who travels a lot?

Buy him something that removes a hassle he hits on every trip. Men who travel often already own a suitcase and a neck pillow; what they notice is engine noise, a dead phone, a lost bag or a bulky wallet. Headphones, AirTags, a power bank and a slim wallet fix those problems, and every pick on this page fits in a carry-on, so he'll actually bring it.

## Can he pack a power bank in his checked bag?

No, a power bank has to go in his carry-on. The FAA says spare lithium batteries and power banks "must be carried with the passenger in carry-on baggage" and are not allowed in checked bags ([FAA](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/lithium-batteries-baggage)). The TSA also lets him bring an empty tumbler like the YETI through security and fill it at the gate, since its liquids rule covers what's inside, not the cup ([TSA](https://www.tsa.gov/travel/security-screening/liquids-aerosols-gels-rule)).

## Which travel gift is the best upgrade?

The Samsonite Freeform carry-on is the biggest upgrade for a man who travels often: a spinner that rolls easily through an airport and fits most overhead bins means fewer checked bags and less waiting at the carousel. For a smaller gift with the same effect, pair the AirTag 4-pack with the Bose headphones.

## Tips for choosing a travel gift

- **Check his phone.** AirTags and the Anker MagGo work best with a recent iPhone.
- **Think about his trips.** Business travelers want the headphones and wallet; road trippers want the speaker and tumbler.
- **Size the luggage.** Airline carry-on limits vary, so check his usual airline before buying a suitcase.
- **Add a small extra.** The Ridge Wallet or YETI tumbler tucks in next to a bigger gift.
