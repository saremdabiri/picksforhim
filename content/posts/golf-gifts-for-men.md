## How to choose a golf gift for him

- **Watch or rangefinder?** A GPS watch is the easy everyday upgrade; a laser rangefinder gives the exact distance to the pin. Golfers who already own one often want the other.
- **Think about the season.** In winter, a putting mat keeps his game sharp at home; in spring, new balls and a cart speaker get used right away.
- **Big trip coming up?** A hard-shell travel case is the gift that protects his clubs on every flight.
- **Buy premium consumables.** Titleist Pro V1 balls are a small luxury golfers love to receive.
