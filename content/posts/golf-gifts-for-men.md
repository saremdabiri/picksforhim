## What are the best golf gifts for men who have everything?

Upgrade what he uses every round instead of adding novelty clutter. A golfer who has clubs and a bag still goes through balls, so a dozen Titleist Pro V1 is always welcome. If he already owns a GPS watch, a laser rangefinder gives exact yardage to the flag, and the reverse is also true. Planning a buddies' trip? A hard-shell travel case protects his clubs on every flight.

## Is a golf watch or a rangefinder the better gift?

A GPS golf watch shows distances to the front, middle and back of every green on his wrist, with no device to pull out of the bag, and it doubles as a fitness tracker. A laser rangefinder like the Bushnell Tour V6 Shift measures the exact distance to the flag or a hazard. Under the [USGA rules on distance-measuring devices](https://www.usga.org/content/usga/home-page/rules-hub/rules-modernization/major-changes/use-of-distance-measuring-devices.html), distance-measuring devices are allowed unless a local rule bans them, so both work in most casual and club play.

## What are good funny golf gifts for a stocking?

Small, useful and a little silly works best. Shanker golf socks, printed funny golf tees and a toilet golf set are bestsellers with thousands of reviews between them, and they're easy to wrap. Pair one with a sleeve of premium balls so the joke gift also gets used on the course.
