## What do you get a dad who has everything for Christmas?

Skip more stuff and pick one of two kinds of gifts. The first is something the family shares, like a digital photo frame that siblings and grandkids keep filling with new pictures, or a fire pit that brings everyone outside. The second is an upgrade to something he already uses every day, like a slim wallet or better whisky glasses. Both get used long after the wrapping paper is gone.

## Is a gift card a good Christmas gift for dad?

It's the most-wanted gift overall: the [National Retail Federation's 2026 holiday data](https://nrf.com/research-insights/holiday-data-and-trends/winter-holidays) puts gift cards first at 47%, then clothing at 44%. But for a dad who already has everything, a gift card often goes unused. A specific upgrade he'd never buy himself, like an Oura Ring or a TheraGun, shows you thought about him and gets used right away.

## What is a sentimental Christmas gift for dad?

A digital photo frame is the easiest sentimental gift to get right. Set up the Aura Carver before you wrap it, invite your siblings in the app, and load it with old family photos, so it's full of memories the moment he plugs it in. Add a handwritten card about a favorite memory together and it becomes the gift he talks about all year.
