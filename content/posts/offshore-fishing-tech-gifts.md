For the man who runs offshore, the best gifts aren't more lures. They're the high-tech upgrades he's been eyeing all season: live sonar, a bigger screen at the helm, an electric reel for deep drops and a satellite lifeline in his pocket.

## How to choose

- **Know his boat.** Electronics must fit his console and work with his current Garmin display. A quick photo of the helm helps.
- **Inshore or deep water?** Electric reels shine for deep-drop trips; sealed spinning reels suit popping, jigging and casting to schools.
- **Safety counts.** If he fishes beyond cell range, a satellite communicator is the gift everyone at home will thank you for.
