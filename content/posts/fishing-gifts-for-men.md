## How to choose

- **Freshwater or salt?** Saltwater gear needs sealed, corrosion-resistant parts.
- **Shore, kayak or boat?** It decides fishfinder size and how much gear he can carry.
- **Upgrade what he uses most.** A better reel or sunglasses get used every single trip.
