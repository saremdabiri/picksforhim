## What do you get a dad who loves fishing?

Start with how he fishes. Boat and kayak anglers get the most from a fishfinder like the Garmin STRIKER, which shows fish, bait and structure below. Shore and pier anglers reach for a smoother reel or polarized sunglasses every trip. If he brings his catch home, a cordless electric fillet knife saves real time at the sink. A YETI cooler is the safe pick if you can't tell.

## Why are polarized sunglasses a top fishing gift?

Sunlight reflecting off water creates glare that hides what is under the surface. Polarized lenses block much of that horizontal glare, so anglers can see fish, weed beds and drop-offs, and their eyes tire less on long days. Pick a wraparound frame like the Costa Brine to block light from the sides, and check that the lenses offer full UV protection.

## Does he need a fishing license to use these gifts?

Probably. Most U.S. states require adults to hold a freshwater or saltwater fishing license, with exceptions such as some private ponds or fishing with a licensed captain, according to [Take Me Fishing](https://www.takemefishing.org/blog/april-2025/a-guide-to-seasonal-fishing-licenses-and-other-license-matters/). Many states offer discounted licenses for seniors, veterans and military members, so check his state wildlife agency before the season starts.
