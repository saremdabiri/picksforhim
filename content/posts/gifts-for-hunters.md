Shopping for a hunter? The best gifts are the ones that keep him warm, hidden and confident through long, cold sits, not another camo mug. These are gear upgrades from brands hunters trust: TIDEWE heated gear and blinds, Vortex optics, Tactacam and Outdoor Edge.

## How to choose a gift for a hunter

- **What does he hunt?** Deer and turkey hunters use ground blinds and trail cameras; duck hunters live in heated gloves and waders.
- **Tree stand or ground?** A ground blind and heated chair suit ground hunters; binoculars, a rangefinder and heated layers help everyone.
- **Check sizes and batteries.** Heated clothing needs the right fit and a charged battery; cellular trail cameras need a data plan.
- **Upgrade what he uses every hunt.** Better binoculars or warmer gloves get used every single trip.
