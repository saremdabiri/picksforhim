## What are the best deer hunting gifts for men?

For deer hunters, the gifts that get used every season are scouting and shot-confidence gear. A cellular trail camera like the Tactacam REVEAL X 3.0 sends photos to his phone, so he can learn where deer move without walking in and leaving scent. Pair it with 10x42 binoculars for glassing field edges, a laser rangefinder for ethical shot distances, and a field dressing kit for after the shot.

## Why is a tree stand harness a smart hunting gift?

Because tree stand falls are a real risk and most injured hunters weren't wearing one. In a University of Wisconsin study of 117 hunters treated after tree stand falls, only four reported using a safety harness and 55 percent had one or more spinal injuries, according to [WPR](https://www.wpr.org/environment/uw-study-falls-tree-stands-most-likely-result-spinal-injuries). Experts recommend a full-body harness with a short tether, worn from the moment he leaves the ground.

## How to choose a gift for a hunter

- **What does he hunt?** Deer and turkey hunters use ground blinds and trail cameras; duck hunters live in heated gloves and waders.
- **Tree stand or ground?** A harness is a must for tree stand hunters; a ground blind and heated chair suit ground hunters; binoculars, a rangefinder and heated layers help everyone.
- **Check sizes and batteries.** Heated clothing and harnesses need the right fit; cellular trail cameras need a data plan.
- **Upgrade what he uses every hunt.** Better binoculars or warmer gloves get used every single trip.
