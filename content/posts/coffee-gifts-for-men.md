## How do you choose a coffee gift for a man?

Start with **how he drinks it**. If he loves espresso, an espresso machine is the big, memorable gift. If he wants speed, a Nespresso or a one-touch De'Longhi is the smarter pick. If he brews drip, French press or pour-over, a burr grinder and a Fellow kettle make the biggest difference.

Then think about **effort**. The Breville machines reward a little practice and tinkering, which a lot of guys love. The De'Longhi machines do everything with one tap.

## Tips for buying coffee gifts for him

- **Add the beans.** Tuck a bag of a local roaster's espresso blend in with the machine.
- **Check the counter.** Espresso machines need space under the cabinets.
