## How to choose a gift for an outdoorsman

Start with what he does most. A hunter spends cold hours sitting still, so heated gear and good binoculars matter. A fisherman lives on the water, so a cooler, waders and a satellite communicator are the upgrades. A hiker or camper will use a GPS watch, a multi-tool and a fire pit every trip.

- **Check his gear first.** Outdoorsmen are picky about brands; a quick look in the garage tells you what he already owns.
- **Think about the season.** For a winter birthday or Christmas, cold-weather gear like the TIDEWE heated jacket gets used right away.
- **Size matters for apparel.** Check his jacket and boot size before buying waders or heated gear.
- **Safety gear is a thoughtful gift.** The inReach Mini 3 Plus gives the people at home peace of mind too.
