## How do you choose a leather gift for a man?

Start with **what he carries every day**. A man who commutes needs a briefcase; a man who travels on weekends will use a duffel more. Then look at **his wallet**: if it's thick and worn out, a slim Bellroy is a real upgrade. For the watch lover, a **watch box** at home and a **watch roll** for trips make a great pair.

## Leather care tips

- **Let it age.** Good leather darkens and softens with use, which is part of the appeal.
- **Condition once or twice a year.** A little leather conditioner keeps bags and wallets from drying out.
- **Keep it dry.** Wipe off rain and store leather away from radiators.
