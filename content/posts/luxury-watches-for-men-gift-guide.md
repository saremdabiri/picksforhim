A fine watch is the rare gift that becomes part of someone's story. It goes to the wedding, the interview, the first day as a father, and one day it's handed down.

You don't need to spend a fortune to give something with real craftsmanship. These five watches come from respected makers with long histories, and each has its own personality.

<!-- list -->

## What to look for

- **Movement.** Automatic for romance, quartz for precision and ease, solar for set-and-forget.
- **Case size.** 38 to 40mm is the classic sweet spot.
- **Bracelet or strap.** Steel bracelets are versatile; leather dresses it up.
- **Water resistance.** Check the rating if he swims or fishes with it on.

## Presentation matters

Hand it over in a watch roll or valet tray and it instantly feels like an occasion. A short handwritten note about why you chose it goes a long way.
