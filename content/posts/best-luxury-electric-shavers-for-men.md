A great shave is one of the few luxuries a man uses every single day, which is exactly what makes a premium electric shaver such a smart gift. The best ones are precisely engineered, built to last for years, and turn a chore into a small ritual.

The two big decisions are **foil or rotary**, and how much refinement you want in the extras: cleaning stations, travel cases and finishes. Here are the shavers worth giving.

<!-- list -->

## What to look for

- **Shaving system.** Foil for close straight passes; rotary for curves and thicker growth.
- **Wet and dry use.** Lets him shave with foam or in the shower.
- **Cleaning station.** Keeps the head performing; budget for cartridges.
- **Battery life and travel lock.** Matters for the man who travels.
- **Replacement parts.** Choose a model whose heads are easy to find.

## The finishing touch

Pair the shaver with a quality pre-shave or post-shave balm and a leather travel case. That's the difference between a gadget and a gift.
