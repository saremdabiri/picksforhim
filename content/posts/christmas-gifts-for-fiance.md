## What is the best Christmas gift for a fiance (male)?

The best Christmas gift for a fiance is one that celebrates being engaged, not just another gadget. A couple's journal like A Year of Us gives you a daily question to answer together through the engagement, and a Tissot PRX is a classic watch he can wear on the wedding day. Engagements are short and busy: [The Knot's research on engagement length](https://www.theknot.com/content/average-engagement-length) shows most couples are engaged for a little over a year, so this is likely your only Christmas as fiances.

## What do you get your fiance for your first Christmas engaged?

For your first Christmas engaged, give one gift that looks back and one that looks ahead. The journal or the What I Love About You fill-in book captures how you feel this year. A gift for your future home, like the Breville Barista Express or a Solo Stove fire pit, is something you'll both use for years. Add a handwritten letter about the proposal; it costs nothing and is often the part he keeps longest.

## What can I get my fiance if we're saving for the wedding?

If you're saving for the wedding, keep the gift small and personal. The What I Love About You book and the A Year of Us journal are budget-friendly and mean more than a pricier gadget. Pair one with an experience you plan yourself, like recreating your first date.
