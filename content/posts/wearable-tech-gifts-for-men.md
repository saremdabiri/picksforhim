## How do you choose a wearable tech gift for him?

Start with **his phone**. An Apple Watch only works with an iPhone; the Pixel Watch 5 is the pick for Android. Garmin watches and the Oura Ring work with both.

Then think about **how he spends his time**. A man who runs, hikes, hunts or fishes gets the most from a Garmin, with its long battery life and maps. A man who wants calls, texts and payments on his wrist is happier with an Apple Watch. A man who hates wearing a watch, especially to bed, will actually keep wearing a smart ring.

## Smartwatch, smart ring or earbuds?

- **Smartwatch:** the most useful all-round gift, with notifications, fitness and payments.
- **Smart ring:** the quietest option, with no screen, focused on sleep and recovery.
- **Earbuds:** the easy add-on, and the one he'll use on every commute and workout.

## Tips for buying wearable tech

- **Check the size.** Watches come in two case sizes; the Oura Ring needs a sizing kit first.
- **Look at subscriptions.** The Oura app needs a membership after the trial; Apple and Garmin don't.
- **Keep the gift receipt.** Every pick here is sold by Amazon.com, so swaps are easy.
