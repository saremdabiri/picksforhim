## How to choose stocking stuffers for a teen boy

Start with one thing he'll use every day, like earbuds, a power bank or a wallet. Then add one fun item, like the LEGO set, so the stocking isn't all practical. If he has an iPhone, the AirTag, MagGo power bank and AirPods all work best with it; for Android, skip those and lean on the wallet, beanie and controller.

Younger teens (13 to 15) love LEGO, gaming gear and a beanie. Older teens (16 to 19) appreciate a first cologne, a Ridge wallet or AirPods. For one bigger gift, put the AirPods in the stocking and keep the rest small.
