## What do men want when they retire?

Time for the things work crowded out, and often sooner than planned. In the 2025 Retirement Confidence Survey from the Employee Benefit Research Institute, the median retirement age reported by retirees was 62, and three in five retirees said they retired before 65 ([EBRI](https://www.ebri.org/crawler/view/2025-ebri-greenwald-retirement-confidence-survey)). That's a lot of good years for hobbies, so a gift that upgrades the one he loves (grilling, golf, fishing, coffee) gets used more than any plaque.
