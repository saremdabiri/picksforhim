## How to choose a gift for a duck hunter

- **Waders first.** Leaky waders end more hunts than anything else. If his are old, new waders are the most useful gift you can give. Insulated bootfoot waders suit cold water; breathable waders suit walking in.
- **Know how he hunts.** Field and goose hunters need a layout or panel blind; marsh and timber hunters care more about waders and a jacket that blends in.
- **Think about the dog.** Many duck hunters care about their retriever's gear as much as their own. A training dummy is an easy win.
- **Don't guess on calls and guns.** Duck calls are personal, and firearms are a gift he should choose himself.
