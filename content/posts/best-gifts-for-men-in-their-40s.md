## How to choose a gift for a man in his 40s

- **Invest in quality.** A Swiss watch or a classic cologne is something he'll keep for years.
- **Feed the hobby.** Golf, grilling, running and coffee are where a premium tool gets used every week.
- **Make travel easier.** Great noise-cancelling headphones are one of the most-used gifts for a man who flies for work.
- **Buy from the brand or Amazon.** For watches and fragrance, the seller matters as much as the product.
