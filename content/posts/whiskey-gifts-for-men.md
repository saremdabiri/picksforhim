## How do you choose a whiskey gift for a man?

Start with how he drinks it. A **neat** whiskey drinker gets the most from Glencairn tasting glasses. A man who takes it **on the rocks** will use crystal rocks glasses and clear ice every night. A **cocktail** guy, especially an old fashioned fan, will love a smoker. And if he hosts, a decanter or the Bartesian is the gift guests notice.

## Decanter, glasses or smoker?

A **decanter** is a display piece: it makes a bar cart or office shelf look finished. **Glasses** are the everyday upgrade he'll use most. A **smoker** is the fun one, the gift that gets pulled out when friends come over.

## Tips for buying whiskey gifts

- **Pair two gifts.** Rocks glasses plus the ice ball mold make a complete set.
- **Go crystal for a keepsake.** Waterford and Riedel are names he'll recognize.
- **Add a bottle yourself.** A favorite bourbon or Scotch turns any of these into a full gift.
- **Gift responsibly.** These are for enjoying a good drink slowly.
