## How to choose a birthday gift for your husband

- **Start with what he does on weekends.** Golf, cooking, music or building things: a premium upgrade for his hobby always lands.
- **Make milestone birthdays a keepsake.** A watch or a fragrance becomes part of his everyday routine and reminds him of the day.
- **Pick something to share.** A fire pit or an espresso machine turns the gift into time together.
- **Buy from Amazon or the brand.** For watches and fragrance, the seller matters as much as the product.
