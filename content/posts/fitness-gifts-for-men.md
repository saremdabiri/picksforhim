## How do you choose a fitness gift for a man?

Start with how he trains. Runners get the most from a GPS watch and open-ear headphones, lifters from a massage gun or adjustable dumbbells, and the guy who's into sleep scores and recovery data will wear an Oura Ring every night. If you're not sure, recovery tech is the safest bet: almost every active man gets sore, and few buy a premium massage gun for themselves.

## Watch, ring or massage gun?

A **watch** is for the man who wants stats during the workout: pace, heart rate, maps. A **ring** is for the man who wants to know how he slept and whether to train hard today. A **massage gun** is for after the workout, and it's the gift other people in the house end up borrowing.

## Tips for buying fitness gifts

- **Check what he already wears.** If he has a Garmin, a newer Garmin beats switching him to another brand.
- **Go one level up.** The premium version of something he already uses is always a hit.
- **Size carefully.** Rings need a size; watches and massage guns don't.
- **Think about space.** Adjustable dumbbells beat a rack in a small home.
