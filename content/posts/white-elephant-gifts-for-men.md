## What makes a good white elephant gift for men?

A good white elephant gift for men works for anyone who might open it and makes someone else want to steal it. In a [white elephant exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_elephant_gift_exchange), players can take an already-opened gift instead of a new one, so the best gifts are things the whole room can picture using, like a s'mores maker or hand warmers, or a gag that keeps the laugh going all night.

## Funny or useful: which white elephant gift wins?

Useful gifts usually get stolen more; funny gifts usually get the bigger laugh. If you're not sure who'll end up with your gift, pick one that does both, like the ToiLight or the back scratcher: they're a joke when they're unwrapped and still useful at home afterward. Save pure gag gifts like The Screaming Goat for groups that know each other well enough to laugh together.

## How do you pick a white elephant gift for a mixed group?

Choose something that would work for a man or a woman, wrap it so the shape doesn't give it away, and stay within your group's spending limit. Food gadgets, winter warmers and small tools are safe bets for mixed groups. For a gift exchange that's only guys, the toilet golf set and the work lights land well.
