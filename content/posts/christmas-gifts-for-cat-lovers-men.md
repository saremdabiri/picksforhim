## What do men who love cats want for Christmas?

Most cat guys want two things: something that makes life with the cat easier, and something that shows off the fact that they're a cat person without being too cute. That's why the best gifts on this list split into practical cat gear (a camera, a heated bed, a fountain) and funny cat dad gifts (a whiskey glass, coasters, a humor book). Cats live in tens of millions of U.S. homes ([Global Pet Industry](https://globalpetindustry.com/news/2010-2025-us-pet-ownership-shifts-after-15-years-of-steady-growth/)), so there's no shortage of cat dads to shop for.

## Should you buy a gift for him or for his cat?

If you're not sure, buy for the cat. A man who loves his cat is usually happier with a gift his cat uses every day than with a novelty item. A no-subscription camera, a heated bed or a stainless fountain are things he might not buy himself. Add one funny piece, like The Catfather glass, so there's something to open that's just for him.
