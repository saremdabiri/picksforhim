## How to choose a leather anniversary gift for your husband

Think about where he spends his day. If he commutes, the briefcase is the gift he'll use most. If you love to travel together, a leather duffel with a weekend trip booked is the most romantic option on this list. If he already has good bags, go small and personal: a wallet or valet tray plus the leather journal with a letter on the first page.

Leather gets better with age, which is exactly why it's the third anniversary gift. Pick full leather over "PU" or "vegan leather" listings, keep the receipt, and add a note: the gift is the leather, the meaning is what you write.
