## What is the best Christmas gift for a grandpa who lives far away?

A digital photo frame is the best Christmas gift for a grandpa who lives far away, because it keeps delivering new photos all year. With the Aura frame, everyone in the family sends pictures from their phones and they show up on his frame automatically. It fits how families already stay in touch: [AARP's 2026 research on grandparents](https://datastories.aarp.org/social-leisure/relationships/the-essential-role-of-grandparents/connections-and-relationships/) found 76% of grandparents say technology such as texting, video calls and shared photo apps is a primary way they stay connected.

## What are good sentimental Christmas gifts for grandpa from the grandkids?

The most sentimental gifts let grandpa share his story or see the grandkids every day. A guided life story journal gives him prompts to write about his childhood, his first job and the advice he'd pass on, and it turns into a family keepsake. Pair it with a frame preloaded with photos of the grandkids, and add a handmade card or drawing; that's usually the part he keeps.

## What should I get a grandpa who says he doesn't want anything?

Get him an upgrade to something he already uses every day. Most grandpas who "don't want anything" still wear slippers, drink coffee and read, so UGG Tasman slippers, a YETI tumbler or a Kindle Paperwhite gets used right away. For the handy grandpa, a small tool like a lighted magnetic pickup makes a smart stocking stuffer.
