## What do teenage boys want for Christmas?

Most teenage boys want the same things their friends use: a console, good earbuds or headphones, and a speaker for their room. Gaming and audio make up most of this list for that reason. If you're unsure, ask a friend or sibling which console and phone he uses, since that decides whether a Switch 2, Meta Quest 3S, Apple Watch or AirTags will work for him. In a 2023 Pew Research Center survey, 97% of US teen boys said they play video games, so a gaming gift is rarely a miss ([Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2024/05/09/teens-and-video-games-today/)).

## What are good Christmas gifts for teenage boys by age?

At 13 to 15, the Nintendo Switch 2 and Beats Solo 4 are favorites, because they're easy to share with friends and survive a backpack. At 14 to 16, add the JBL Flip 7 or Apple Watch SE 3 for sport-minded teens. At 16 to 18, practical gifts land: AirPods 5 for school and work, an AirTag 4-pack for keys and a car, or a Kindle Paperwhite for the commute to college.

## How to choose a gift for a teenage boy

- **Ask what his friends use.** Teens care about the same console or earbuds as their group.
- **Check compatibility.** Apple Watch and AirTags work best with an iPhone; the Meta Quest needs a phone app for setup.
- **Think about where he'll use it.** Earbuds for school and sport, a speaker for the room, a console for weekends.
