## How to choose a gift for a teenage boy

- **Ask what his friends use.** Teens care about the same console or earbuds as their group.
- **Check compatibility.** Apple Watch and AirTags work best with an iPhone; the Meta Quest needs a phone app for setup.
- **Think about where he'll use it.** Earbuds for school and sport, a speaker for the room, a console for weekends.
