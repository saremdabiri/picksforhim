## How to choose a luxury gift for a man

- **Buy for his hobby.** Golf, training, music or the outdoors: the premium version of the gear he already uses always lands.
- **Pick something he'll use daily.** A watch, headphones or a cologne gets used every day, so the splurge pays off.
- **Go for the name he respects.** Garmin, Sonos, YETI and Armani are brands men recognize and trust.
- **Check the seller.** For watches and fragrance, buy from Amazon.com, the brand or an established dealer, never an unknown reseller.
