## How to choose a luxury gift for your husband

- **Upgrade the daily ritual.** Coffee, his commute, the watch on his wrist. The flagship version of something he uses every day gets noticed every day.
- **Feed the hobby.** Grilling, running, hosting. Buy the centerpiece, not another accessory.
- **Think about the occasion.** A watch suits an anniversary or a big birthday; fragrance and leather make a lovely second gift at Christmas.
- **Check the details.** Wrist size for a watch, iPhone or Android for headphones, and any membership a smart device needs.
