## How to choose a gift for the husband who has everything

- **Upgrade, don't add.** Look at what he uses daily (headphones, cologne, his watch) and give him the version he'd call too expensive.
- **Give him an experience at home.** A fire pit or a LEGO build creates evenings, not clutter.
- **Comfort wins in winter.** Heated gear and recovery tools get used every week until spring.
- **Check the seller.** For watches, fragrance and tech, buy from Amazon.com or the brand, never an unknown reseller.
