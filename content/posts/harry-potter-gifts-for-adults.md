## How do you choose a Harry Potter gift for an adult?

Start with **how he enjoys the series**. A LEGO builder will love a big display set; a reader will treasure the illustrated edition; a collector wants an official **Noble Collection** replica for the shelf. Then think about **space**: the Great Hall and Diagon Alley need a real shelf, while the Castle and Grounds model fits on a desk.
