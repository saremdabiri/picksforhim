## How to choose a cologne for a man

- **Fresh or warm?** Fresh scents (Acqua di Giò, Luna Rossa Ocean) suit daytime and the office; warm, spicy scents (Spicebomb Extreme, Jazz Club, Code Parfum) suit evenings and winter.
- **Eau de parfum lasts longer.** Parfum and eau de parfum are more concentrated than eau de toilette, so a few sprays go further.
- **Pick a crowd-pleaser if unsure.** The most-reviewed colognes here have thousands of happy buyers for a reason.
- **Only buy from the brand or Amazon.com.** Designer fragrance is often counterfeited; every pick in this guide is sold by the brand or by Amazon itself.
