## What do you buy a gamer husband who has everything?

Upgrade the gear he touches every session instead of buying another game. A pro controller, a better headset or a lighter mouse changes how every game feels, and most gamers put off buying them for themselves. If he truly has all of that, go sideways: the Meta Quest 3S adds VR without needing a console or PC, and the BACKBONE Pro lets him play on his phone while traveling.

## Is the DualSense Edge worth it as a gift?

Yes, if he plays on PS5 often. PlayStation says the DualSense Edge adds back buttons (half-dome and lever styles come in the box), swappable stick caps and control profiles he can switch between without leaving the game ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/accessories/dualsense-edge-wireless-controller/)), and its stick modules can be replaced if they ever drift. It's a premium controller, so it suits the husband who games several nights a week, not the once-a-month player.

## Tips for buying a gift for a gamer husband

- **Find out his system.** Look at the console under the TV or ask what he plays on; PS5, Xbox, Switch and PC gear don't all mix.
- **Upgrade what he uses most.** Controller for console players, mouse and headset for PC players.
- **Keep the box and receipt.** Gamers can be picky about gear, so make returns easy.
- **Add a stocking stuffer.** The headphone stand or a digital gift card for his platform rounds out the gift.
