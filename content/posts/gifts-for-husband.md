Shopping for your husband is hard: he says he doesn't need anything, and he buys what he wants himself. The secret is to upgrade the things he already uses every single day, or feed the hobby he loves.

These are the gifts husbands actually use, from Christmas morning to his birthday to your anniversary.

## How to choose a gift for your husband

- **Upgrade a daily habit.** Shaving, music, coffee, his commute. The flagship version of something he uses every day gets noticed.
- **Feed the hobby.** Grilling, fishing, golf, fitness. Go one level above what he'd buy himself.
- **Make a milestone mean something.** For an anniversary or a big birthday, a watch lasts for decades.
