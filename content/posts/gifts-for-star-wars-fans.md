## How do you choose a gift for a Star Wars fan?

Start with **his era**. Original trilogy fans light up for Vader, the Falcon and Imperial walkers. Prequel and Clone Wars fans love clone troopers. Newer fans watched The Mandalorian, so the Razor Crest or N-1 is the hit. Then look at **his shelf**: if he already displays LEGO, an Ultimate Collector Series set fits right in; if not, a helmet makes a bigger first impression.

## LEGO set or Black Series helmet?

A **LEGO set** is a gift he gets to build, then display. A **Black Series helmet** is ready to show off the moment it comes out of the box and makes a better centerpiece. For a big Christmas gift, pairing a small LEGO set with a helmet works well.

## Tips for buying Star Wars gifts

- **Buy popular sets early.** LEGO Star Wars sets often sell out in December.
- **Check what he owns.** Ask a friend or sneak a look at his shelf before you buy.
- **Go adult.** Look for the 18+ display sets, not the kids' play sets.
