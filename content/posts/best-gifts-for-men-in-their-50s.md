## How to choose a gift for a man in his 50s

- **Mark the milestone.** For a 50th birthday, a Swiss watch or a signature cologne is something he'll keep and remember.
- **Upgrade the hobby.** Golf, grilling and the backyard are where a premium tool gets used all year.
- **Think comfort and recovery.** Sleep tracking and compression boots are gifts he may not buy for himself.
- **Buy from the brand or Amazon.** For watches and fragrance, the seller matters as much as the product.
