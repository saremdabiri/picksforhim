Anniversary gifts for husband should feel personal and last longer than the dinner reservation. The best ones are pieces he'll use or wear every day and think of you when he does: a fine watch, a signature cologne, or a luxury upgrade he'd never buy himself.

## How to choose an anniversary gift for your husband

- **Pick something that lasts.** Watches, cologne and quality gear are reminders he uses daily.
- **Match the milestone.** A first anniversary can be playful; a 10th or 25th calls for a watch or something he'll keep for decades.
- **Think about what you do together.** Travel, golf, the gym or the water: a gift that fits your shared life lands best.
- **Add a personal note.** A short handwritten card turns a great product into a great anniversary gift.
