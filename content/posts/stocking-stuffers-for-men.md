## How to choose stocking stuffers for him

- **Think everyday carry.** Keys, wallet, earbuds, coffee: upgrade the things that are already in his pockets.
- **Choose a premium brand in a small size.** A TheraGun Mini or a Leatherman says more than a pile of gadgets.
- **Mix useful with fun.** One practical piece (AirTags, a tumbler) plus one treat (cologne, earbuds) makes a great stocking.
- **Order early.** The best-selling items sell out first in December.
