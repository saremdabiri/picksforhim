## How do you choose a cocktail gift for him?

Start with how he drinks. A man who **hosts** gets the most from a Bartesian, because it makes a round of drinks fast. A man who likes the **craft** of mixing will use professional bar tools and a smoker every weekend. A **wine** drinker will get more from a Coravin than from any shaker.

## Machine, tools or smoker?

A **cocktail machine** is about speed and consistency. **Bar tools** are about the ritual of making a drink by hand. A **smoker** is the fun one, the gift he pulls out when friends come over.

## Tips for buying cocktail gifts

- **Pair two gifts.** Bar tools plus the clear ice mold make a complete set.
- **Check his counter.** The Bartesian Professional is large; the Duet fits almost anywhere.
- **Add a bottle yourself.** A favorite bourbon or gin turns any of these into a full gift.
- **Gift responsibly.** These are for enjoying a good drink slowly.
