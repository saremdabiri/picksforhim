## Christmas gifts for dad from daughter: what makes them special?

A gift from a daughter usually works best when it carries a memory as well as a use. A watch like the Seiko Presage Cocktail Time is the classic keepsake, since an automatic watch can be passed down. A Kindle Paperwhite becomes personal when you load the books you've talked about, and a ThermoWorks Thermapen ONE is a thoughtful pick for the dad who cooks every family dinner. Add a handwritten card; it's often the part he keeps.

## Christmas gifts for dad from son: what does dad actually want?

Most dads want time and a better version of their favorite gear. Sons tend to share a hobby with Dad, so feed that: a Traeger Pro 22 for backyard cookouts, a Bushnell Tour V6 Shift rangefinder for the course, a Garmin STRIKER Vivid 7cv fishfinder for the boat or a YETI Tundra 45 for tailgates. The best version of this gift comes with a plan: book the tee time or the fishing trip and go with him.

## What do you get a dad who wants nothing?

When dad says he wants nothing, he usually means he doesn't want more stuff. So skip novelty gadgets and upgrade a thing he already reaches for every week: the Kindle Paperwhite if he reads in bed, the Bushnell rangefinder if he golfs, the YETI Tundra 45 if he tailgates or fishes, or the Braun Series 9 PRO+ for his morning shave. Useful beats clever.

If he truly means it, pair a small upgrade with time together: a round of golf, a fishing trip or a cookout where he runs the new Traeger.

## How to choose a Christmas gift for dad

- **Upgrade, don't add.** The flagship version of something he already uses beats another gadget for the drawer.
- **Feed the hobby.** Grilling, golf, fishing or fitness. Go one level above what he would buy himself.
- **Think long-term.** Watches, coolers and tough gear last for decades and carry memories.
