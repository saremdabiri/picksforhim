## How do you pick a Christmas gift for your brother?

Start with how he spends his free time, not with what's on sale. A brother who games gets the most out of a VR headset or a LEGO build, a brother who's always outside wants a cooler or a fire pit, and a brother who's always at the gym will use a smartwatch or a massage gun every week. If you're splitting a gift with siblings, pool your budget on one premium pick instead of three small ones.

## Gifts for older brother vs. younger brother

Older brothers with a house and a family tend to love gifts for hosting and the backyard: the Solo Stove, the YETI or the Bose speaker. Younger brothers usually go for the fun stuff: the Meta Quest 3S, the Millennium Falcon or a new watch. A good fragrance works for both.

## Tips for buying for a brother

- **Ask his partner or a parent.** They usually know what he's been eyeing.
- **Go one level up.** Pick the premium version of something he already uses.
- **Skip sizes.** Clothing is risky; tech, gear and fragrance aren't.
- **Add a note.** A short card about a shared memory makes any gift land.
