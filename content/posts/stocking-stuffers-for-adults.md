## How to choose stocking stuffers for adults

- **Upgrade something they already use.** Earbuds, a wallet, a phone battery or a favorite cologne get used daily, so a premium version is always appreciated.
- **Mix one big surprise with smaller picks.** A single bigger item, like AirPods Pro 3 or a Kindle, makes the whole stocking feel special.
- **Think pocket-sized.** If it fits in a coat pocket, it fits in a stocking: speakers, multitools and slim wallets are ideal.
