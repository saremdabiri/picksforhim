## What should a wife get her husband for Christmas?

Think about what he uses every single day and buy the best version of it. Most husbands won't spend on premium headphones, a Swiss watch or a top-tier shaver for themselves, but they use them daily once they have one. That's why AirPods Max 2, the Tissot PRX and the Braun Series 9 PRO+ are on this list. If you two love game nights, a Nintendo Switch 2 is a gift you'll both use.

## What if my husband has everything?

When he already owns the gadget, upgrade the experience around it. Recovery boots like the Hyperice Normatec Elite, a new cologne for date nights, or shearling-lined UGG slippers are luxuries most men never buy for themselves. Pair the gift with a plan, like a weekend away or a dinner you book, and he gets something to open and something to look forward to.

## How to choose a Christmas gift for your husband

- **Go one level above what he'd buy.** He has a watch, a cologne, slippers. Give him the version he'd never pick for himself.
- **Surprise him with new tech.** Smart glasses and the new Apple Watch Ultra are things many men want but haven't bought.
- **Think about how he spends his weekends.** Training, fishing, grilling or golf: feed the hobby with gear he'll use for years.
- **Order early.** Popular gifts sell out in December, and Prime delivery slows down close to Christmas.
