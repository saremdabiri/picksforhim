The best gifts for men aren't gimmicks. They're the upgraded version of something he already loves: the morning shave, the watch on his wrist, Saturday at the grill, Sunday on the water.

This is our Christmas edit: refined, well-made gifts from brands with real reputations, chosen to be used for years.

## How to choose

- **Upgrade the everyday.** The gifts that get used are the ones that get remembered.
- **Feed the passion.** Grill, fishing, travel: buy the centerpiece, not the accessories drawer.
- **Mind the details.** Sizes, finishes and compatibility (for example, his boat or his grill fuel).
