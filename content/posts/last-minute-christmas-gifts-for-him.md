## When is it too late to order Christmas gifts for him?

It's later than most people think. For the lower 48 states, the [USPS 2026 holiday shipping dates](https://about.usps.com/newsroom/national-releases/2026/0922-usps-recommends-2026-holiday-mailing-shipping-dates.htm) recommend sending by Dec. 17 for Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail, Dec. 18 for Priority Mail and Dec. 19 for Priority Mail Express. If you're ordering on Amazon, trust the delivery date shown under the price on the product page, and order a few days before it to be safe.
