## How to choose a Christmas gift for your boyfriend

- **Match it to how you spend time together.** Gaming nights, gym sessions or coffee runs: the premium version of what he already does always lands.
- **Pick something he'll use daily.** A watch, headphones or cologne gets used every day, so the splurge pays off.
- **Early in the relationship?** Keep it personal but not over the top: a cologne, slippers or a LEGO build. Together a while? A watch or headphones says you pay attention.
- **Check the seller.** For watches and fragrance, buy from Amazon.com or the brand, never an unknown reseller.
