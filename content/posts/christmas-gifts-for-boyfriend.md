## What do you get your boyfriend for Christmas?

Get him the premium version of something he already uses every day. That's why watches, headphones and cologne top most gift lists: he'll use them daily, so the splurge pays off, and he'll think of you each time. Look at how you spend time together, too. If it's gaming nights, a Switch 2 is a shared gift; if it's the gym or weekend runs, a Garmin or TheraGun fits his routine. Novelty gifts get a laugh on Christmas morning and then sit in a drawer.

## What if you've only been dating a few months?

Keep it personal but not over the top. A cologne, cozy slippers or a LEGO build says you pay attention without the weight of a watch. A good rule many couples use: match the spend to how long you've been together and what you've talked about, and agree on a budget if you're unsure. Together a while? That's when a watch or premium headphones lands best.

## How to choose a Christmas gift for your boyfriend

- **Match it to how you spend time together.** Gaming nights, gym sessions or coffee runs: the premium version of what he already does always lands.
- **Pick something he'll use daily.** A watch, headphones or cologne gets used every day, so the splurge pays off.
- **Check the seller.** For watches and fragrance, buy from Amazon.com or the brand, never an unknown reseller. The FTC also suggests checking the seller and the return policy before you buy online ([FTC online shopping tips](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/online-shopping)).
- **Order early.** Popular consoles and headphones can sell out in December; Amazon shows the live price and delivery date on each product page.
