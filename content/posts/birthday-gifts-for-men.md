A great birthday gift for a man is something he'd want but wouldn't buy himself. Start with what he does on weekends: golf, fitness, grilling, music. Then go one level up.

Here are the birthday gifts men actually want this year, for your husband, boyfriend, dad or brother.

## Birthday gift tips

- **Match the hobby.** A golfer, a runner and a griller want very different things.
- **Go premium on the small stuff.** Earbuds, a shaver or a thermometer he'll use every week.
- **Add a card with a plan.** Pair the rangefinder with a tee time, the smoker with a cookout.
