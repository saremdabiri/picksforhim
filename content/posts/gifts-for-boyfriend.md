## What are good sentimental gifts for boyfriend?

The best sentimental gifts for a boyfriend carry your own words or shared memories. A What I Love About You book you fill in by hand, a couple's journal like A Year of Us (4.6 stars, 7,000+ reviews) or an engraved wallet card with a short note all cost little but mean a lot. [Research from Carnegie Mellon](https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2017/07/170725100701.htm) found recipients value sentimental gifts more than givers expect, so don't skip the personal touch.

## What should I get my boyfriend for Christmas?

For Christmas, pair one practical gift with one personal one. AirPods Pro 3 or a Tissot PRX are the safe, high-impact picks he'll use every day; a Nintendo Switch 2 or Meta Quest 3S win with gamers. Then add something small and sentimental, like a filled-in What I Love About You book or an engraved wallet card, so the gift feels like it could only have come from you.

## How to pick

- **New relationship?** Keep it fun and low-pressure: a LEGO build, a question journal or a small engraved card.
- **Been together a while?** A watch or premium earbuds, plus a handwritten note, is a gift with meaning.
- **Know his hobby?** Upgrade his gear for it. That's the gift he'll talk about.
