## How do you choose a garage gift for a man?

Start with **what he actually does out there**. A man who works on his own car wants the mechanics set and the jump starter. A DIY dad who builds and fixes things around the house gets the most out of the DEWALT combo kit. If his garage is already full of tools, the best gift is **storage**: a rolling tower or tool box makes the space feel new.

Then check **his battery platform**. Cordless tools only share batteries within one brand, so look at what's on his shelf before you buy a battery tool.

## Tips for buying garage gifts

- **Big set over small extras.** One complete set beats a handful of single tools he may already own.
- **Think safety.** A jump starter and a floodlight camera are gifts he'll be glad to have on a cold night.
- **Buy early.** Tool sets and DEWALT kits often get their best prices before Christmas.
