## How do you choose a smart home gift for a man?

Start with **his ecosystem**. If he talks to Alexa, Ring and the Echo Show 15 fit right in. If he uses Google Home, the Nest thermostat is the natural pick. Then check **his home**: wired doorbells, floodlights and thermostats need the right wiring, so they suit homeowners more than renters. Speakers, the Echo Show and the myQ add-on work almost anywhere.

## Smart home or garage gift?

A **smart home gift** saves him a little time every day. A **garage gift** is for the man who fixes things himself. The best Christmas combo is one of each: a Floodlight Cam over the garage and the DEWALT kit to install it.

## Tips for buying smart home gifts

- **Check compatibility.** Make sure the thermostat works with his heating system and the doorbell matches his wiring.
- **Stick to one platform.** Gifts that work with what he already owns get set up; the rest stay in the box.
- **Buy early.** Ring and Echo devices often get their best prices before Christmas and sell out in December.
