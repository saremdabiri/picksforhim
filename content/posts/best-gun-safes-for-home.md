Responsible ownership starts with secure storage. The right safe keeps firearms out of the wrong hands, especially children's, while still giving you fast access when it counts.

We've gathered the safes worth owning: quick-access biometric models for the nightstand, smart safes for a collection, a cabinet for long guns, a travel case and a fireproof safe for the papers and valuables that matter.

<!-- list -->

## What to look for

- **Access type.** Fingerprint is fastest; keypad and mechanical locks are reliable backups.
- **Steel thickness and build.** Heavier gauge resists prying.
- **Mounting.** A safe that can be bolted down is far harder to steal.
- **Fire rating.** Quick-access safes rarely have one; add a fire-rated safe for documents.
- **Size.** Count what he owns today, then leave room for one more.

## Safe-storage habits

- Store ammunition separately where required.
- Bolt safes to the floor, wall or furniture.
- Check local and state safe-storage laws.
- Change batteries on a schedule, such as when you change clocks.
